Cybersecurity

We find security gaps and help close them, in office networks and in the control systems that run plants, water and utilities (ICS/OT). We work to NIST frameworks and write plans sized to your organization.

When to call us

  • You need a vulnerability assessment or a penetration test.
  • You need an incident response plan, or need to test the one you have.
  • You handle criminal justice data and need CJIS-aligned controls.
  • A defense contract needs a NIST SP 800-171 self-assessment.
  • Your control systems are connected to the office network.
  • You want to know your backups actually restore.

What you get

  • An assessment report sorted by risk, with fixes in order.
  • Test results.
  • Plans and policies written for your size, not a template.
  • A recheck after the fixes are in.

How it runs

  1. First call.
  2. Written scope, including what we will and will not touch.
  3. Testing in agreed windows.
  4. Report and walk-through.
  5. Recheck.

Questions

Will testing take our systems down?

We agree in writing what we test and when. Anything that could interrupt service is scheduled with you.

Do you test disaster recovery?

Yes. We run a restore and tell you how long it took and what was missing.

Do you provide managed IT?

No. We work alongside the company that runs your IT.
