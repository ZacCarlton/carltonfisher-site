AI Embedment

We put AI into the tools you already use: reading forms, sorting requests, searching your own records, flagging problems in data. Each use is written down: what it does, what data it sees, how it was tested, and who checks its output.

For Kansas agencies we work to ITEC Policy 6200, including disclosing our AI use and supporting your security review.

When to call us

  • Staff spend hours reading or sorting things that follow rules.
  • You want to try AI but need to show how it was checked.
  • A vendor is selling you an AI feature and you want it tested before you buy.
  • Your agency has to meet ITEC 6200.

What you get

  • A short written plan.
  • The working tool, inside your systems.
  • Test results on your own data, including how often it is wrong.
  • A record of what the AI does and does not do.
  • Instructions for your staff.

How it runs

  1. First call.
  2. We look at the task and tell you if AI is worth it. Sometimes it is not.
  3. Written scope and estimate with a ceiling.
  4. A small trial on real data, measured.
  5. Roll-out only if the trial holds up.

Questions

Will our data train someone else's AI?

No. We set it up so your data is not used for training, and we show you the setting.

What happens when the AI is wrong?

Where a mistake matters, a person checks the output before anyone acts on it. We measure the error rate in testing and tell you the number.
