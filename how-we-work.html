How we work

The first call

30 minutes, free, by Google Meet or phone, weekdays 9 to 4 Central. Bring whatever you have: an RFP, a problem in a few sentences, a list of your systems. If we are not the right fit, we will say so on the call.

Scope and estimate

For most jobs, within 5 business days of the call you get a written scope and an estimate with a ceiling. For an RFP, we follow its schedule. We do not go over the ceiling without your written OK.

Small first jobs

We can size a first job small. Many cities and the state have simpler buying rules for small professional-service jobs, and a small job is a good way to find out whether we fit.

The work

You see working progress every two weeks. We keep written records as we go. The code and documents are yours from the first day.

Build or check, never both

On any one system, we either build it or independently check it. If we built it, someone else verifies it. If we verify it, we did not build it.

Your data

Your data stays in your systems or in a shared workspace we set up with you, where you can see everything we hold. None of it goes into an AI tool without your written OK. At the end we return it or delete it, your choice.

AI in our own work

We sometimes use AI tools for drafting, and we tell you where.

Is this consulting?

Yes, if you mean paying for our judgment. We also do the work ourselves and hand you what we built.
