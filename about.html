About

Why Carlton Fisher

Carlton Fisher puts two careers together. Marcus spent more than 28 years making sure software worked on NASA missions before it flew. Zac has delivered more than 200 building projects that had to pass inspection. We started the company to bring that standard to software and systems work for public agencies and private companies.

Marcus Fisher

Marcus has spent more than 28 years leading software and systems work for NASA, for state government projects and for private industry. His work includes contributions to the Artemis lunar missions and the James Webb Space Telescope. He wrote Software Verification and Validation (Springer, 2007). He holds an active TS/SCI clearance.

Zac Carlton

Zac Carlton is a residential developer, builder and designer, and a multifamily and commercial contractor, with more than 200 completed projects. At Carlton Fisher he leads business development and client work. He's usually your first call.

The firm

Carlton Fisher is two people, based in Dodge City, Kansas. We size the team to the job: the two of us on a small job, and people we have worked with before on a larger one. You'll meet our technical team before they start. We work with private industry and with local, state and federal government.

The first call is 30 minutes and free.
