Systems Integration

We connect systems that were bought separately so data moves between them without anyone keying it in twice. That can be a plant's controllers reporting into its ERP or quality software, a county system feeding a state system, or a new system replacing an old one without losing data. We write down every connection so the next person can see how it works.

On state and federal projects we also do integration and testing oversight: we review the integration and test plans and watch the testing on the owner's behalf.

When to call us

  • Two systems hold the same data and your staff enter it twice.
  • You are replacing a system and need the data and connections moved.
  • A project has several vendors and nobody owns how the pieces fit together.
  • Equipment on the plant floor (PLCs) needs to report into ERP or quality software.

What you get

  • A map of your systems and the data that moves between them.
  • A written description of each connection.
  • The connecting code or configuration.
  • Test results that show data arrives complete and correct.
  • Handover and instructions.

How it runs

  1. First call.
  2. We map what you have before we propose anything.
  3. Written scope and estimate with a ceiling.
  4. We build and test one connection at a time.
  5. Handover.

Questions

Do you replace our systems?

Usually not. Most of the time the job is to connect what you have.

Do you model systems before building?

When the project calls for it, yes, including SysML models.

Can you oversee another vendor's integration work?

Yes. That is often a better use of us than building it.
