  1. A free 30-minute call. Bring what you have. If we are not the right fit, we say so.
  2. A written scope and an estimate with a ceiling, within 5 business days for most jobs. For an RFP, we follow its schedule.
  3. The work, with working progress every two weeks. The code and documents are yours from the first day.

On any one system, we either build it or independently check it. Never both.
